Building the Trust Layer for Internet Commerce.

People can already discover each other globally on X, Discord, and Telegram, and move value in seconds. Yet, trusting internet strangers with digital payments remains broken. Baxis provides the non-custodial trust infrastructure that makes online deals safe.

Why Baxis Exists

Internet Commerce Lacks a Universal Trust Layer

Transacting on the internet should not require blind faith. Freelancers deliver quality work and get ghosted without payment; clients send upfront deposits only to be scammed.

The Asymmetric Risk Problem

Without an intermediary, either the buyer or the seller takes 100% of the financial risk. Direct wallet transfers leave buyers unprotected, while unpaid work leaves freelancers helpless.

Centralized Platforms Are Broken

Legacy freelance platforms force discovery onto closed networks, charge 10–20% commission fees, enforce slow payouts, and rely on centralized accounts that can freeze funds arbitrarily.

The Problem

Blockchain Solves Payments, Not Trust

Cryptocurrency solved global peer-to-peer value transfer, but raw transactions are final and irreversible. Baxis bridges the gap between payment rails and deal execution.

Irreversible Transfers

Sending crypto directly to a wallet leaves no recourse if the counterparty fails to deliver agreed assets or code.

Unresponsive Clients

Freelancers are often left hanging after completing work. Baxis implements cryptographic auto-release inspection windows.

Arbitrary Disagreements

When disagreements occur, strangers need an impartial, evidence-based dispute mechanism rather than chaotic public arguments.

Mission

Our Operational Mission

Safe Agreements

Objective contract terms with defined deliverables and timelocks agreed upon before work begins.

Transparent Payments

Funds are deposited directly on-chain where both parties can independently verify the vault balance.

Decentralized Escrow

Non-custodial smart contracts hold deposits—preventing third parties from moving funds arbitrarily.

Fair Arbitration

Impartial dispute resolution based strictly on submitted proof assets and contract terms.

Workflow

How Baxis Works

STEP 01

Create Agreement

Set contract terms, deposit amount, and counterparty wallet address in seconds.

STEP 02

Fund Escrow

Client locks USDC stablecoins directly into the non-custodial smart contract vault.

STEP 03

Complete Work

Freelancer submits deliverables and proof assets into encrypted vault storage.

STEP 04

Release Funds

Client approves payout, or auto-release executes upon inspection timelock expiry.

Architecture

Why We Chose Web3 Infrastructure

We chose smart contracts on the blockchain because decentralized infrastructure provides security capabilities that traditional bank rails cannot match:

Transparent Settlements

Contract balances and token movements are publicly verifiable on Base scan, eliminating accounting ambiguity.

Programmable Escrow Logic

Timelocks, inspection windows, and release conditions are encoded strictly into smart contract bytecode.

Immutable Records

Once an escrow is funded on-chain, transaction logs cannot be altered, backdated, or deleted by any single entity.

Global Accessibility

Anyone with an EVM wallet can participate without geographic banking restrictions or high wire fees.
