Guidelines

Logo Usage Rules

Correct Usage (Do)
  • Maintain minimum padding around the logotype equal to half the height of the wordmark.
  • Use the primary dark logo on black/dark canvas surfaces (#090909, #111111).
  • Use the inverted white logo on light background surfaces.
  • Ensure the wordmark remains crisp and legible at small display sizes.
Incorrect Usage (Don't)
  • Do not stretch, distort, rotate, or skew the logotype proportions.
  • Do not recolor the logotype with non-brand primary colors or drop shadows.
  • Do not place the logotype over busy, low-contrast background photography.
  • Do not refer to Baxis as a "freelance agency" or "marketplace". Baxis is non-custodial trust infrastructure.
