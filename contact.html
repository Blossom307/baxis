Support FAQs & SLA

Where can I report a bug?

Technical software bugs can be submitted via the contact form above under "Technical Support" or opened as an issue on our GitHub repository.

How do security disclosures work?

Security researchers should email security@baxis.io directly with reproduction steps. Reports are acknowledged within 12 hours.

How do partnership inquiries work?

We collaborate with DAOs, freelancing networks, and Web3 tool builders seeking non-custodial escrow integrations.

What is the response time for support?

General support tickets are addressed within 24 hours. Critical security vulnerability disclosures receive priority response within 12 hours.
