Frequently Asked Questions

Every Baxis escrow includes an automated timelock (e.g., 48 hours). If deliverables are submitted and the client fails to review or respond before the timer expires, the contract auto-releases payment to your wallet.

Baxis natively supports Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Solana, and Polygon. You can transact in stablecoins like USDC and USDT, as well as native ETH and SOL.
