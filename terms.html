4. Escrow Process & Smart Contract Usage

Baxis operates using non-custodial smart contracts deployed on the Base blockchain (`BaxisEscrow.sol`).

  • Funding Vaults: When a client funds an escrow via `fundEscrow`, stablecoin funds (USDC) are locked directly into smart contract code—not company bank accounts.
  • Inspection Timelocks: Every vault includes an agreed inspection window (`MIN_INSPECTION_WINDOW` = 24 hours to `MAX_INSPECTION_WINDOW` = 90 days).
  • Automated Releases: Clients can approve payouts manually (`releaseFunds`). If the inspection window expires after work submission without a dispute being raised, freelancers can claim automated payouts (`claimInspectionPayout`).

8. Protocol Fees

Baxis collects a default platform fee (e.g., 3.0% / 300 basis points) upon escrow funding to sustain network infrastructure and arbitration services. The smart contract enforces a hard-coded maximum fee cap of 10.0% (`MAX_FEE_BPS = 1000`). Fee structures may evolve as protocol governance matures.

9. Dispute Resolution & Official Arbitration

If a party initiates a dispute via `raiseDispute`, funds inside the smart contract freeze in `DISPUTED` status. Both parties agree to submit justifications and evidence files through the Baxis workspace. Designated Baxis Arbitrators review off-chain proof and execute binding on-chain settlements (`resolveDispute`), distributing funds between client and freelancer in accordance with submitted evidence.
